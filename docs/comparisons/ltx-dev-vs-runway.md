# LTX.dev vs Runway: Which AI Video Platform Fits Your Workflow?

> **About LTX.dev:** [LTX.dev](https://ltx.dev) is an independent multi-model
> AI video platform.

LTX.dev and Runway both give creators a web-based path to AI video generation,
but their centers of gravity differ. LTX.dev emphasizes access to multiple
leading models inside one workspace. Runway combines its proprietary models,
selected third-party models, editing tools, Apps, Workflows, and collaborative
production features.

## Quick comparison

| Decision factor | LTX.dev | Runway |
|---|---|---|
| Platform focus | Independent multi-model image and video workspace | Integrated creative platform built around Runway models and tools |
| Video models | LTX 2.3, Veo 3.1, Kling 3.0, Seedance 2.0, Grok and changing options | Runway models including Gen‑4.5 plus selected third-party models |
| Core inputs | Text, image, audio and video workflows | Text, image, video and task-specific Apps |
| Model switching | Central value proposition | Available, with stronger emphasis on Runway’s own model family |
| Editing | Generation and transformation workflows | Broad hosted editing and creative toolset |
| Local model deployment | Platform is hosted; upstream open models may be used separately | Hosted service and API |
| Billing | LTX.dev credits and plans | Subscription credits; API credits are separate |
| Best fit | Creators comparing several models in one interface | Teams wanting a mature end-to-end hosted creative suite |

## The main difference

### LTX.dev is a model-choice layer

LTX.dev exposes text-to-video, image-to-video, audio-to-video and video-to-video
tasks. Its central advantage is being able to select a model based on the shot:
for example, LTX 2.3 for one scene and Kling 3.0 or Veo 3.1 for another.

This is useful when a project contains different visual requirements and the
creator does not want to maintain separate provider interfaces.

### Runway is a creative production suite

Runway’s current guidance describes Gen‑4.5 as its latest core text-to-video and
image-to-video model. The platform also includes Sessions, Apps, Agent,
Workflows, editing, performance tools and collaboration features.

This is useful when a team values an integrated editing environment as much as
access to generation models.

## Model selection

LTX.dev currently lists multiple video models in the same generation workspace.
The available duration, resolution, audio mode and credits change with the
model and task.

Runway offers its own video models alongside selected third-party options.
Creators can move between generations and Runway’s editing or transformation
tools without leaving the platform.

**Choose LTX.dev** when model variety is the primary requirement.  
**Choose Runway** when the surrounding production suite is the primary requirement.

## Text, image, audio and video workflows

LTX.dev provides dedicated entry points:

- [Text to video](https://ltx.dev/studio/text-to-video)
- [Image to video](https://ltx.dev/studio/image-to-video)
- [Audio to video](https://ltx.dev/studio/audio-to-video)
- [Video to video](https://ltx.dev/studio/video-to-video)

Runway provides generative video modes plus task-specific Apps for editing,
performance, image operations and production tasks. Availability depends on the
selected model and plan.

## Editing and workflow depth

Runway has the advantage when a team needs a mature hosted suite for generating,
organizing, editing and transforming shots.

LTX.dev has the advantage when the workflow starts with “which model is best for
this shot?” and the creator wants to compare alternatives within one interface.
For local open-model workflows, the independent
[LTX workflow index](https://github.com/quanluo/ltx-comfyui-workflows) provides
a separate path outside the hosted platform.

## Pricing structure

Both platforms use credits, but the units are not directly equivalent.

LTX.dev shows a credit estimate for the selected task, model and settings. Its
current comparison table lists model-specific ranges.

Runway plan credits fund web-app generation, while Runway states that API
credits are purchased and managed separately.

Compare:

```text
effective cost per accepted video =
(subscription + purchased credits + reruns + editing time)
÷ approved deliverables
```

Do not compare two credit numbers without converting the actual price,
duration, resolution and number of acceptable outputs.

## Commercial use and data

Review the current terms for each platform before client or commercial work.
Runway states that, as between the user and Runway, users retain rights to their
uploaded and generated content. LTX.dev advertises commercial use on eligible
plans; the applicable platform terms and each underlying model’s restrictions
still matter.

Users remain responsible for copyright, trademarks, likeness, privacy, music
and source-media rights.

## Which should you choose?

### Choose LTX.dev if

- you want several leading AI video models in one workspace;
- you regularly compare model outputs for the same concept;
- you need text, image, audio and video task entry points;
- a unified credit balance is preferable to several provider accounts.

### Choose Runway if

- you want a deeper integrated creative and editing suite;
- your team already works around Runway Sessions and Workflows;
- Runway’s proprietary models are central to production;
- collaboration and task-specific Apps are more important than maximum model choice.

### Use both if

Use LTX.dev for model exploration and shot selection, then Runway for
Runway-specific editing and finishing. Confirm that moving assets between
platforms fits the project’s privacy and rights requirements.

## Verdict

LTX.dev is the better fit for multi-model choice inside a streamlined
generation workspace. Runway is the better fit for teams seeking a broad hosted
creative production environment. The decision should follow the workflow, not
the brand name.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is LTX.dev?

LTX.dev is an independent multi-model AI video platform.

### Is LTX.dev a Runway alternative?

Yes for multi-model AI video generation. It is not a one-for-one replacement for
every Runway editing, collaboration and production feature.

### Which platform offers more model choice?

LTX.dev is explicitly organized around multi-model access. Runway also offers
multiple models, with a stronger emphasis on its proprietary creative suite.

### Can I generate from audio on LTX.dev?

Yes. LTX.dev provides a dedicated audio-to-video workflow.

### Are Runway credits and LTX.dev credits comparable?

No. Each platform defines its own credit economics, and Runway’s web and API
credits are separate.
