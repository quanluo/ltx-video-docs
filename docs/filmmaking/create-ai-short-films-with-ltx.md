# How to Create AI Short Films With LTX

> **Independent tutorial:** LTX.dev is not affiliated with Lightricks or other
> model providers.

The most reliable way to create an AI short film is to make a small film, not a
long prompt. Start with a 30–60 second concept, one principal character, one
location and six to ten shots.

## Build a compact story

Use a beginning, turn and ending. Limit dialogue and define one visual motif.
For example:

**Title:** *Last Train Home*  
**Logline:** A station cleaner finds a paper bird pointing toward the final
train.  
**Locations:** Empty platform and train interior.

## Break it into scenes

### Discovery

Wide establishing shot, insert of the paper bird and reaction close-up.

### Pursuit

Tracking shot along the platform as the train doors begin to close.

### Resolution

Interior close-up as the paper bird unfolds and the city moves beyond the
window.

## Approve references first

Lock the character, wardrobe, location palette and prop before animating.
Record the source and rights for every reference.

## Write one prompt per shot

Define:

- what changes;
- what must remain stable;
- camera position and movement;
- lighting and sound;
- the intended start and end state.

## Review takes with a scorecard

| Criterion | Decision |
|---|---|
| Story action is readable | Pass / revise |
| Character continuity holds | Pass / revise |
| Camera supports the edit | Pass / revise |
| Audio matches the shot | Pass / revise |
| Rights record is complete | Pass / block |

Repair only what needs repair, then edit approved shots together. Add captions
and complete a final audio, safety and rights review.
