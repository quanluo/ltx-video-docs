# AI Video Model Benchmark 2026: Published Specs Compared

> **About LTX.dev:** [LTX.dev](https://ltx.dev) is an independent multi-model
> AI video platform.

AI video “benchmark” pages often mix laboratory tests, marketing claims, and
different product tiers. This comparison takes a narrower and more reproducible
approach: it organizes currently published specifications and the model options
shown in LTX.dev’s live selector. It does not invent visual-quality scores.

## Quick comparison

The following snapshot reflects model options and credit ranges published by
LTX.dev on 30 July 2026. Availability and pricing can change.

| Model | Duration shown on LTX.dev | Resolution shown | LTX.dev credits shown | Useful starting point |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| LTX 2.3 | 5–10 seconds | 720p–1080p | 10–50 | Fast text/image-to-video and synchronized audio |
| Veo 3.1 | 8 seconds | 720p–1080p | 30–150 | Cinematic prompting and audio-capable generation |
| Kling 3.0 | 10–15 seconds | 720p–1080p | 20–100 | Longer shots, references and multi-shot workflows |
| Seedance 2.0 | 5–12 seconds | 720p–1080p | 20–96 | Flexible social, narrative and motion workflows |
| Grok | 5–8 seconds | 720p–1080p | 15–60 | Short-form video experimentation |

Credits are platform units, not a universal price-per-second measurement.
Different task types, model variants, resolutions, and durations can use
different amounts.

## What the table can—and cannot—tell you

Published specifications help answer:

- whether a model supports the desired clip length;
- which resolution options are exposed by the platform;
- whether a model is available for a task;
- the approximate credit range inside one interface;
- whether audio or reference inputs are part of the workflow.

They cannot establish:

- which model has the “best” visual quality;
- which model follows every prompt most accurately;
- real queue time at a specific hour;
- cost per usable output;
- consistency across every subject and style.

For production selection, use the published table to create a shortlist, then
compare two or three models with the same prompt and input assets.

## Model-by-model notes

### LTX 2.3

LTX 2.3 is available in the LTX.dev text-to-video workspace with Pro and Fast
paths. The upstream model family is known for joint audio-video generation,
image-to-video, local model access, and workflow integrations. LTX.dev provides
hosted access and is separate from the upstream model owner.

[Generate with LTX 2.3](https://ltx.dev/studio/text-to-video/ltx)

### Veo 3.1

Veo 3.1 is positioned for high-quality video generation with audio-capable
workflows. It is a useful candidate for cinematic shots and scenes where sound
is part of the prompt. Exact capabilities depend on the endpoint and mode made
available by the platform.

[Open the multi-model text-to-video workspace](https://ltx.dev/studio/text-to-video)

### Kling 3.0

Kuaishou’s official Kling 3.0 announcement describes multimodal inputs,
reference-to-video, in-video editing, native audio, and generation up to 15
seconds. LTX.dev currently exposes Kling 3.0 as one of its selectable video
models.

[Generate with Kling 3.0](https://ltx.dev/studio/text-to-video/kling)

### Seedance 2.0

Seedance 2.0 appears in the LTX.dev model selector for video generation. Its
published duration range makes it suitable for evaluating medium-length shots,
social content, and narrative sequences alongside Kling and Veo.

[Compare models in LTX.dev](https://ltx.dev/studio/text-to-video)

### Grok

Grok is available as a short-form video option in the current LTX.dev selector.
It can be included when comparing rapid iterations and shorter clips against
other hosted models.

## A practical source-based scoring method

Create a decision score from requirements rather than subjective hype:

| Requirement | Weight example | How to score |
|---|---:|---|
| Required duration | 20% | Does the available mode meet the shot length? |
| Required input type | 20% | Text, image, audio, video, or reference support |
| Audio requirement | 15% | Native audio, supplied audio, or silent output |
| Resolution | 10% | Does the exposed mode meet delivery requirements? |
| Credit range | 15% | Estimated platform cost for the chosen settings |
| Workflow fit | 10% | Can the output feed the next production step? |
| Commercial terms | 10% | Do platform and model terms fit the project? |

The best model is the one that clears mandatory requirements with the lowest
acceptable cost and review burden.

## Recommended model-selection workflow

1. Choose the task: [text to video](https://ltx.dev/studio/text-to-video),
   [image to video](https://ltx.dev/studio/image-to-video),
   [audio to video](https://ltx.dev/studio/audio-to-video), or
   [video to video](https://ltx.dev/studio/video-to-video).
2. Define duration, aspect ratio, audio, and reference requirements.
3. Shortlist the models that expose those controls.
4. Reuse the same prompt and input asset across the shortlist.
5. Compare prompt adherence, motion, continuity, sound, and usable seconds.
6. Save the selected model and settings as a reusable workflow.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best AI video model in 2026?

There is no universal winner. LTX 2.3, Veo 3.1, Kling 3.0, Seedance 2.0, and
Grok expose different durations, inputs, audio capabilities, and cost ranges.
Choose by task and compare the same scene across a shortlist.

### Which model supports the longest clip on LTX.dev?

In the published comparison snapshot reviewed on 30 July 2026, Kling 3.0 shows
a range of 10–15 seconds, the longest maximum among the five listed options.

### Does LTX.dev own the models it offers?

LTX.dev is an independent multi-model AI video platform.

### Can I compare models with the same prompt?

Yes. A shared interface makes it easier to reuse a prompt and inputs, although
model-specific controls may still differ.

### Are LTX.dev credits the same as provider API credits?

No. They are platform-specific units and should not be compared directly with
another provider’s credits without converting the actual settings and price.
