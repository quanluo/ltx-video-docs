# Cinematic AI Video Prompts for LTX-2.3

> **About LTX.dev:** [LTX.dev](https://ltx.dev) is an independent multi-model
> AI video platform.

Useful cinematic prompts describe filmmaking intent rather than stacking
quality adjectives. Organize them by camera, lighting, action and sound.

## Slow dolly-in

> A tired night-shift baker stands beside a cooling rack before sunrise. Flour
> hangs faintly in the air as he removes one final loaf and exhales. The camera
> begins in a medium-wide shot and performs a slow, straight dolly-in. Cold blue
> window light mixes with a warm oven glow; metal trays settle with soft clicks.

## Lateral tracking shot

> A woman in a yellow raincoat walks beside a moving tram through a foggy
> station. The camera tracks parallel at waist height, keeping her centered
> while columns pass in the foreground. Footsteps, rail noise and a distant
> announcement remain synchronized.

## Crane reveal

> A small fishing boat leaves a dark harbor at dawn. The camera begins low
> behind coiled ropes, then rises smoothly above the pier to reveal open water
> and a pale horizon. Gulls cross the frame as the engine grows louder.

## Product macro orbit

> A silver wireless earbud case rotates slowly on black glass. A thin rim light
> traces its edge as the lid opens. The camera performs a controlled macro
> orbit; a clean mechanical click accompanies the movement.

## Suspense follow shot

> A maintenance worker walks through a dim service corridor carrying a
> flickering flashlight. The camera follows three steps behind. Steam escapes
> from a pipe ahead as a distant metal impact stops the worker in place.
