# Prompt Synchronized Audio and Video

Status: editorial guide based on official LTX-2 prompt structure; prompts were
not independently generated by LTX.dev.

LTX-2 jointly generates audio and video. A useful prompt places visible causes
and audible results in the same chronological sequence.

## Recommended structure

1. Establish the subject and setting.
2. Describe the first visible action.
3. Name the sound caused by that action.
4. Continue with the next motion and sound.
5. State camera movement and distance.
6. Add ambience that should remain continuous.
7. Write dialogue exactly and identify tone/speaker when needed.

## Example

> A baker places a metal tray on a stone counter with one solid clack. She opens
> the oven; the hinge creaks and hot air moves her apron. The camera slides
> closer as she lifts a loaf, taps the crust twice, and says in a quiet satisfied
> voice, "That is the sound we wanted." A ventilation fan remains softly audible
> throughout.

The visual event and sound are adjacent: tray/clack, door/creak, tap/tap,
speaker/dialogue.

## Dialogue tips

- Keep one speaker per short shot when possible.
- Put spoken words in quotation marks.
- Describe tone without relying on a named real person.
- Avoid contradictory mouth actions and off-screen speech.
- Obtain consent for real voices or identities.

## Common prompt problems

- listing sound effects separately from the action;
- requesting several simultaneous camera moves;
- using abstract quality adjectives instead of concrete events;
- overcrowding a short shot with many speakers and location changes;
- asking for exact copyrighted music or a real person's voice.
