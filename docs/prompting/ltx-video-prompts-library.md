# Practical LTX Video Prompt Library

> **About LTX.dev:** [LTX.dev](https://ltx.dev) is an independent multi-model
> AI video platform.

LTX prompts are most useful as clear, chronological shot descriptions. Begin
with the main action, then describe movement, appearance, environment, camera,
lighting, color and sound.

## Prompt formula

```text
Main action → movement → appearance → environment
→ camera → lighting and color → sound → ending beat
```

## Cinematic example

> A low tracking shot follows a red bicycle rolling through a narrow market
> street just after rain. The rider’s coat moves in the breeze as vendors close
> striped awnings. Warm signs reflect in the wet pavement. The camera stays one
> meter behind the rear wheel, then rises gently to reveal the street ahead.
> Bicycle-chain clicks, distant conversation and dripping water form the
> soundtrack.

## Product example

> A matte black travel mug stands centered on a pale stone table. A narrow beam
> of morning light moves across its textured surface while the camera performs
> a slow clockwise orbit at tabletop height. A hand enters from the right,
> lifts the mug and twists the lid open with a soft click.

## Image-to-video example

Describe the desired motion rather than repeating the source image:

> The subject turns slowly toward camera and takes one measured step forward.
> Loose fabric moves in a light crosswind while the camera makes a subtle
> dolly-in. Preserve the subject’s clothing, face and color palette.

## Common failures

- **Too many cuts:** generate separate shots and edit them together.
- **Conflicting camera directions:** choose one primary camera movement.
- **Abstract adjectives:** replace “epic” with visible action, framing, light
  and sound.
- **Unrecorded settings:** save the selected model, prompt, duration,
  resolution and any seed or pipeline controls.
